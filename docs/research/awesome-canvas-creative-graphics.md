## Synthesis — Which PASS candidates most improve the arena without weight

For the 960×600 vanilla canvas in `js/render.js`, the highest-ROI **PASS** trio that adds visible punch with near-zero weight is **Proton (or tsParticles bundle, but Proton is lighter), noisejs, and Pts.js/Rough.js as stylistic swaps**. Proton (14kB) replaces the ad-hoc particle array for asteroid explosions and ship thruster with emitter behaviours (alpha/scale/velocity) and handles pooling; noisejs (3kB) supplies seeded Perlin/Simplex to crater asteroid polygons and to dither the starfield/parallax without textures; Pts.js (35kB) or Rough.js (9kB) each give one-line procedural asteroid geometry ( Pts `Create.polygon` + `Pt` math, Rough `rc.polygon` with hachure) and clean trail logic (`globalCompositeOperation` fade) — both are single-file, CORS *, MIT/Apache-2.0, and drop in via `<script>` on GH Pages with no build. tsParticles is kept as a “heavy but pretty” variant alternative, while p5.js/PixiJS are `NEEDS_CHECK` due to LGPL/size and should only be used for throwaway `?variant=` experiments, not the default.
