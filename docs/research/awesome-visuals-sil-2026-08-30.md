## Appendix C — ARCHIVED / NOT ADR-COMPLIANT FOR MAIN — `prototype/graphics-variants` only

> **Why these remain foreclosed on `main` even though many PASS the generic Inclusion Checklist (License MIT, CORS `*`, keyless, ToS, zero cost):** ADR-0001 locks `main` to a single `CanvasRenderingContext2D` owned by `js/render.js` with invariants `setupHiDPI` DPR cap `2`, `seamCopies(x,y,r)→4× ±W/±H` wrap (up to 4 `save/translate/rotate/restore` draws per entity), throttling `frameBudgetMs 20` / `maxStepsPerFrame 20000`, and cached `rough._roughDrawable` hachure.
